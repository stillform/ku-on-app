## Our Mission
We believe in creating calm spaces for everyone. No pressure. No perfection. Just a moment to breathe.

## Why kū-on?
The name comes from the Japanese concept of "calm emptiness" (空穏) — a quiet place where anxiety fades. It's a reminder that you don't have to fight your feelings; you can simply let them be.

## Who This Is For
Anyone who needs a moment to breathe. No experience needed. Just you.

## How It Works
- **Breathe**: Guided breathing exercises with calming audio and gentle visuals
- **Ground**: The 5-4-3-2-1 sensory exercise to bring you back to the present
- **Settle**: A body scan to release tension, one part at a time

## A Note on Privacy
Your sessions are stored only in your browser. No data is sent anywhere. This is your space.
