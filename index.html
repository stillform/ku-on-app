About kū-on

A little quiet when you need it.

kū-on is a private set of short exercises for moments when anxiety, shame, anger, resentment, or overwhelm feels hard to hold. Breathe, ground, release tension, or take one small step through what is happening.

No accountNothing to maintainNothing to get right
For the moment you’re in

Begin wherever you are.

If you know what might help, go straight there. If you do not, choose what feels loudest and kū-on will offer one small place to start.

Use one exercise or stay a little longer. Everything after the first step is optional.

Why it exists

“I made kū-on for the moments when I needed help before I could explain what was wrong.”

A personal projectI wanted something I could open immediately, use without preparation, and leave without turning it into another obligation. The aim is deliberately small: help make the next few minutes feel more manageable.
What you can expect

Designed to ask very little of you.

Open and begin

No account or setup is required. You do not need to name the feeling perfectly before you start.

Private by default

What you enter is not saved, uploaded, or sent by kū-on. Your difficult moments remain yours.

No performance

No scores, streaks, or pressure to continue. Stopping after one breath is still using the app as intended.

空穏kū-on · roughly “koo-on”
The name

A name for openness and calm.

kū-on is a coined name, written symbolically as 空穏. It is not an established Japanese word. The characters were chosen for the ideas they hold here.

空 kūSky, space, or emptiness
穏 onCalm, gentle, or peaceful

Together, they point to the feeling behind the project: a little more room around whatever is happening.

Take only what helps

You do not have to solve everything right now.

Use one exercise, stay for a few minutes, or leave whenever you want.
